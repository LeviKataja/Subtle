Proofreading

A second pass that reads the whole translation at once and corrects only the lines that need it: mistranslated idioms, grammar slips, a name or term translated two ways. The Proofread button on a file's review screen runs it once and shows the cost first.

Proofreading pays off when the reader is stronger than the translator: Haiku translating and Sonnet reading costs less than half of what Sonnet translating does. A reader weaker than the translator can undo good lines.

Cache

Finished chunks are remembered, so repeating a file costs nothing.

Automation

Translate new downloads without being asked. Sonarr or Radarr report an import, Bazarr gets the first chance to find real subtitles, and whatever is still missing is translated and saved next to the video.
